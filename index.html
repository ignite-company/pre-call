The questions I hear most

Let's clear a few things up before the call.

Here are straight answers to the things remodeling owners usually want to understand before investing in better systems.

What exactly does Ignite Co. do?+

We assess your lead-to-customer process and build or improve the systems that help capture inquiries, respond quickly, follow up, reactivate past contacts, request honest reviews and create referral opportunities. That may include a website, qualification funnel, CRM or automation, depending on what you already use and what actually needs fixing.

Are you just another marketing agency?+

No. We're not simply selling ad management or promising more leads. Our focus is the process behind the marketing: getting inquiries to the right person, following up consistently, and improving how your business handles opportunities. Advertising may be part of a separately agreed plan if it makes sense.

Would I have to replace my existing software?+

Not automatically. If your tools are working, the first question is what we can improve around them. If a platform change or integration is recommended, we'll explain the tradeoffs and agree on the scope first.

How much does it cost? Is there a monthly fee?+

The investment depends on the systems you already have and the buildout you need. We discuss the scope and price openly on the call before you make a decision. Optional ongoing support, advertising spend and third-party software charges are separate unless your agreement says otherwise.

Will I own the systems you build?+

You'll keep ownership of the deliverables defined in your agreement. We'll be clear about any tools or subscriptions required to run them, and what happens after the initial buildout.

Can you guarantee more booked jobs?+

Results depend on your existing lead volume, local market, project fit, response times and sales process. We won't invent a return or promise a specific number of projects before looking at your business. The call is where we'll identify the opportunities we can realistically address.

What will we actually do on the call?+

We'll learn how your company currently gets inquiries, responds, follows up and stays in touch with past customers. Then we'll discuss the biggest gaps, whether Ignite is a fit, and what a practical buildout could look like. It's a business conversation, not a generic software demo.
